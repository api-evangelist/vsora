# VSORA

> VSORA is a French fabless semiconductor company (Meudon-La-Foret, France) that designs high-performance AI inference chips. Its Jotunn 8 data-center inference chip (3200 Tflops fp8, 288 GB HBM, TSMC 5nm) targets cost- and energy-efficient large-scale AI inference, and its Tyr family (Tyr 2, Tyr 4) targets edge AI / on-device processing. VSORA is a hardware company and publishes no public web API, developer portal, SDK registry packages, or machine-readable API specifications as of 2026-07-21.
